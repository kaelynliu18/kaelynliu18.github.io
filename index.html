MDTLA

Researcher @ UAB AI.MED Lab

Multi-Scale Digital Twin for Liver Aging

  • 98.3% MASLD classification accuracy, n = 9,200
  • 26.9% lower onset-time RMSE than XGBoost

Fatty liver disease affects 25–40% of Western adults and often goes unnoticed until it's too late. My framework turns a patient's multi-omics and EHR data into a digital twin of their liver. The twin combines mechanistic ODEs, stochastic cell-state transitions and agent-based simulation to predict whether the disease will develop, and when, to within 0.95 years (RMSE).

  • MATLAB
  • Python
  • R
  • ODE models
  • Markov chains
  • Agent-based modeling
  • XGBoost

ePill

Solo developer

Automatic medication logging

  • +3.7% accuracy over a YOLOv10 baseline
  • 2,452 pill classes supported

Overview: Medication errors contribute to an estimated 7,000 to 9,000 deaths annually in the U.S. alone, and negatively affect over 7 million people. ePill automates the pill-tracking process, using deep learning to ensure the correct pills are taken at the right time, offering a solution that is faster and more reliable.

Training: Fine-tuned Gemini 2.5 Flash on synthetic images generated using LAB color-space thresholding and Poisson-disk sampling to outperform a YOLOv10 baseline.

SecondServe

Solo developer

Tennis player digital twin

  • 4.3× faster optimization (9 cores)

A physics-based digital twin of a tennis player. A 3D athlete modeled in MuJoCo searches for motion parameters with CMA-ES, an evolutionary optimizer, within the body's physical constraints. Parallelizing fitness evaluation across nine CPU cores makes each optimization 4.3× faster.
